This Privacy Policy describes how Durbay Otomotiv Mimarlık İnşaat Ticaret Limited Şirketi ("we", "us") handles your information in the Rotascore mobile application and on rotascore.com.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in (with Apple or Google), we receive your name, email address and a unique user ID through Firebase Authentication. Creating an account is optional.
  • Favorites and preferences. Teams, leagues, players and matches you follow, and your notification preferences, so we can sync them across your devices when you are signed in.
  • Device push token. A token issued by Apple/Google so we can deliver the notifications you request (for example goal alerts).
  • Approximate location (country). We derive your country from your IP address to show you a relevant default feed (for example local leagues). We never collect your precise location.
  • Usage and diagnostics data. If you allow app analytics, anonymous interaction events are processed through Google Firebase Analytics. Crash logs and performance diagnostics help us fix bugs and improve the app. If you allow website analytics, Google Analytics also processes page paths, referring pages, browser/device information, country-level approximate location derived from your IP address and interaction data. This data is not linked to your Rotascore account. App analytics is off by default and controlled in Settings → About → Privacy; website analytics is controlled through Cookie preferences in the site footer.
  • Advertising and device data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address and country-level location, device or advertising identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, ad impressions/clicks, and diagnostic or performance data to deliver, measure and protect ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or limited/non-personalized. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking is requested separately through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Purchase and subscription data. To provide, restore and synchronize Rotascore Pro, Apple App Store or Google Play provides the product purchased, transaction and subscription status, expiration information and a RevenueCat App User ID. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; neither Rotascore nor RevenueCat receives your payment-card details.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not require you to allow cross-app tracking to use Rotascore; if you decline, the app continues to work and Google serves ads without access to the IDFA.
  • We do not receive or store your payment-card details.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or messages.

How We Use Information

  • To provide live scores, statistics and the features you use;
  • To deliver the notifications you opt in to;
  • To sync your favorites across devices (signed-in users);
  • To personalize your default content by country;
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance and quality;
  • To verify, activate, restore and support your Rotascore Pro subscription, including ad-free access across supported devices;
  • To fund the free service by delivering, measuring and preventing fraud in advertising, subject to your consent and privacy choices.

Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we process your personal data on the following bases: your consent (for personalized advertising, advertising cookies/identifiers, analytics and diagnostics where consent is required, and for optional push notifications); performance of a contract (to provide account sign-in, cross-device sync and the features you request); and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, debugging and improving Rotascore and in delivering limited/contextual advertising and preventing ad fraud where permitted, balanced against your rights and freedoms. You may withdraw consent or object at any time as described under "Your Rights".

Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to operate and fund the app and website: Google Firebase (authentication, push notifications, optional app analytics, crash reporting — Firebase privacy), Google Analytics (optional website analytics, loaded only after consent — Google privacy), Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (advertising, consent collection, measurement and fraud prevention — how Google uses information from partner apps), Google's disclosed advertising partners selected in our consent message, RevenueCat (subscription entitlement verification, restoration and customer support — RevenueCat privacy), Apple App Store and Google Play (payment and subscription processing), Apple Push Notification service (iOS notifications and Live Activities), and our own hosting providers for application data. Advertising providers may act as independent controllers for their own advertising purposes as described in their policies and in the partner list shown in the in-app privacy message. If Rotascore is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will notify you of any change in ownership or use of your data.

International Data Transfers

Our providers (including Google, Apple and RevenueCat) may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, the UK or Turkey to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards — principally the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and equivalent measures under the UK GDPR and the Turkish KVKK) — so that your data remains protected.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Rotascore app uses mobile SDK identifiers and similar technologies for advertising as described above. No AdMob/UMP initialization or ad request is made during the first app session. From the second session, where required, Google UMP asks for your choices before ads are requested; limited/non-personalized ads can still use information needed for delivery, frequency capping, security and fraud prevention. App analytics remains off by default and may be requested separately only after at least the third session and meaningful use of the app. The rotascore.com website always uses strictly necessary browser storage to remember your TR/EN language and analytics-consent choices. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Allow analytics”; if allowed, it may set analytics cookies such as _ga to distinguish visits and measure website usage. No advertising storage or Google Signals is enabled by this website tag. You can allow, reject or withdraw website analytics at any time through “Cookie preferences” in the site footer. The site also loads the Archivo web font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address solely to serve the font. Within the app, analytics and advertising choices can be changed from Settings → About → Privacy; iOS tracking permission can also be changed in the iOS Settings app.

Data Retention & Deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from the app (Profile → Account → Delete Account), through our account-deletion page, or by emailing us at help@rotascore.com. Anonymous diagnostics data is retained for a limited period by our providers and then deleted automatically. Subscription records may be retained as required by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for accounting, fraud prevention, customer support and legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR, US state privacy laws and the Turkish KVKK), you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; rectify inaccurate data; erase your data; restrict or object to certain processing; receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format (data portability); withdraw consent; and opt out of the sale/sharing or use of personal data for targeted advertising where applicable. We do not sell personal data for money. Use the in-app advertising privacy choices entry point when shown, the applicable consent/privacy message, or email us at help@rotascore.com. On iOS, you can also deny or revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing. We respond to rights requests within the legally required period and free of charge. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in the EU/EEA your national Data Protection Authority, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), and in Turkey the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Children

Rotascore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated in the app.

Web analitiği Rotascore hesabınızla ilişkilendirilmez. Bölgenize ve seçimlerinize göre reklamlar kişiselleştirilmiş veya sınırlı/kişiselleştirilmemiş olabilir. iOS'ta uygulamalar arası izlemeye yönelik reklam tanımlayıcısı erişimi ayrıca Apple ATT istemiyle sorulur.

Abonelikler ve RevenueCat: RevenueCat, satın alma yetkisini doğrulamak, reklamsız Pro erişimini etkinleştirmek, satın alımları geri yüklemek ve abonelik desteği sağlamak için abonelik verilerini veri işleyen hizmet sağlayıcı olarak işler. Uygulama hedefleme için yalnızca düşük hassasiyetli uygulama dili, seçili içerik ülkesi ve uygulama sürümünü RevenueCat'e iletebilir. Ödemeyi Apple veya Google işler; Rotascore ve RevenueCat ödeme kartı bilgilerinizi almaz.

Yapmadıklarımız: Verilerinizi para karşılığı satmayız; ödeme kartı bilgilerinizi almaz veya saklamayız; uygulamayı kullanmak için uygulamalar arası izleme izni vermenizi zorunlu tutmayız; izni reddetseniz de uygulama ve reklamlar IDFA erişimi olmadan çalışmaya devam eder. Hassas konum, rehber, fotoğraf veya mesajlarınızı toplamayız.

İşleme dayanağı (GDPR): Verilerinizi açık rızanıza (gereken yerde kişiselleştirilmiş reklam, reklam tanımlayıcıları, analitik/teşhis ve isteğe bağlı bildirimler), sözleşmenin ifasına (hesap girişi, cihazlar arası senkron ve talep ettiğiniz özellikler) ve meşru menfaatlerimize (izin verilen yerlerde sınırlı/bağlamsal reklam sunmak, reklam sahtekârlığını önlemek, uygulamayı işletmek, güvenliğini sağlamak, hata ayıklamak ve iyileştirmek) dayanarak işleriz; rızanızı dilediğiniz an geri çekebilirsiniz.

Hizmet sağlayıcıları ve yurt dışı aktarım: Firebase, Google Analytics, Google AdMob/Google Mobile Ads ve UMP, RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play, Apple Push Notification service ve uygulama barındırma sağlayıcılarını kullanırız. RevenueCat abonelik doğrulama, geri yükleme ve müşteri desteği için kullanılır (RevenueCat gizlilik politikası). Sağlayıcılarımız verileri ABD dahil ülke dışındaki sunucularda işleyebilir. AEA/İngiltere/Türkiye'den yeterlilik kararı bulunmayan ülkelere yapılan aktarımlarda Avrupa Komisyonu Standart Sözleşme Hükümleri ve KVKK'daki eşdeğer güvenceler uygulanır.

Reklam ve analitik sağlayıcıları ile seçimleriniz: Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads ve User Messaging Platform'u reklam, rıza yönetimi, ölçüm ve sahtekârlık önleme için kullanırız. İlk uygulama oturumunda AdMob/UMP başlatılmaz ve reklam isteği gönderilmez. İkinci oturumdan itibaren gereken bölgelerde Google UMP reklam isteğinden önce seçimlerinizi sorar. Uygulama analitiği varsayılan kapalıdır ve en erken üçüncü oturum ile anlamlı uygulama kullanımından sonra ayrı olarak istenebilir. Seçimlerinizi uygulama içindeki Ayarlar → Hakkında → Gizlilik bölümünden; iOS izleme iznini ayrıca iOS Ayarları'ndan değiştirebilirsiniz. rotascore.com, TR/EN dil ve analitik izin tercihlerinizi hatırlamak için zorunlu depolama kullanır. Google Analytics etiketi yalnızca “Analitiğe izin ver” seçeneğini seçerseniz yüklenir ve kullanım ölçümü için _ga gibi analitik çerezleri kullanabilir; web etiketi reklam depolamasını ve Google Signals'ı etkinleştirmez. Footer'daki “Çerez tercihleri” üzerinden web analitiğine izin verebilir, reddedebilir veya verdiğiniz izni geri çekebilirsiniz. Site ayrıca Google Fonts'tan yazı tipi yükler.

Saklama ve silme: Hesap verileriniz hesabınız aktif olduğu sürece saklanır. Abonelik kayıtları Apple, Google ve RevenueCat tarafından muhasebe, sahtekârlık önleme, destek ve yasal yükümlülükler için gerekli olduğu sürece saklanabilir.
